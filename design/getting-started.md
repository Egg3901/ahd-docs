# Getting Started

Welcome to **A House Divided** — a real-time political simulation where you build a career from the ground up. The world never stops: every hour is a game turn, elections are always in progress, bills move through legislatures, and NPPs (Non-Player Politicians) keep seats warm when players aren't around.

## Choose Your Country

When you create a character, pick a country. Each one has its own electoral system and government structure.

| Country           | System                          | Offices available                                |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| 🇺🇸 United States  | FPTP, separated powers          | House, Senate, Governor, State Senate, President |
| 🇬🇧 United Kingdom | Multi-seat proportional Commons | MP (House of Commons), Prime Minister            |

You can only influence politicians and send funds **within your own country**.

## First Steps

1. **Create your character** — Choose a name, home state (US) or home region (UK: ENG / SCO / WAL / NIR), and policy positions on the economic and social axes (−5 to +5)
2. **Join a party** — Required before you can run for office. Independents can still campaign and fundraise, but can't enter primaries
3. **Build your base** — Campaign to grow Political Influence, fundraise, build a donor network, and run advertisements
4. **Enter a primary** — Declare candidacy during the primary phase; the highest primary score per party advances to the general
5. **Win the general** — Vote accumulation over multiple turns determines the winner; sustained campaigning matters
6. **Hold office** — Earn bonus actions and fund generation each turn, vote on legislation, and face re-election

## Your Two Resource Pools

You have **two separate money pools** — they don't mix:

| Pool               | What it is                    | How you spend it                                   |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| **Campaign Funds** | Your main political war chest | Ads, polls, donor base, NPP influence, party taxes |
| **Cash on Hand**   | Personal liquid cash          | Wire transfers to other politicians only           |

Campaign Funds are built by fundraising and per-turn generation. Cash on Hand is gained separately and is used for person-to-person wire transfers from the Portfolio page.

## Key Stats

- **Political Influence (0–100)** — Your footprint in your home state. Decays each turn without campaigning. Drives vote reach and primary scores in state-level races.
- **Favorability (0–100)** — Public approval. Starts at 50. Stable at or below 60 unless attacks or other hostile effects push it down. Drives vote appeal in all races.
- **National Political Influence (NPI)** — Uncapped. Accrues passively (+state influence / 100 per turn). Only used in **presidential races**.
- **Infamy (0–100)** — Reputation damage from attacking others. Above 20% it drains your Favorability; it also makes your own attacks more likely to backfire. Decays 5% per turn.
- **Actions** — Your activity budget. Base 4/turn; office holders earn more. Accumulate up to 200 (hoarding penalty above 100).
- **Party Influence (0–100)** — How active and aligned you are within your party. Earns you **bonus actions every turn** proportional to your share of the party's total influence pool. See [[Parties]] for the full mechanic.

## Joining a Party — More Than Just a Ballot Line

Party membership does more than let you run in primaries:

- **Party Influence** builds passively when you hold party-related actions and invest in your standing. Higher party influence = more bonus actions per turn from the party pool.
- The **party treasury** can fund NPP influence operations, benefiting all members.
- Party leadership (Chair, Vice Chair, Treasurer) is elected by members every 96 turns — something to aim for later.

## First Week Checklist

If you're just starting out:

- [ ] Campaign a few times to build Political Influence (1 action each, free)
- [ ] Fundraise early from your starting donor network (3 actions, earns $60k at level 1)
- [ ] Build a Donor Network at level 2 when you can afford it to scale future fundraising
- [ ] Run ads when you can afford them to push Favorability above 60%
- [ ] Commission a Quick Poll (2 actions + $25k) before entering any race to check your standing

## More to Explore

Once you have a character:

- **Send mail** — Message other characters directly from their profile page. Your inbox and sent box live on the Notifications page under the Mail tab. Supports **bold** and _italic_ formatting.
- **Support or attack other politicians** — From any character's profile, spend 2 actions to boost or damage their Favorability. Attacking adds Infamy to you.
- **Barnstorm for an ally** — Spend 5 actions + $100,000 to directly boost another politician's Political Influence (+1%, or +2% in their home state).
- **Influence NPPs** — Use NPP profile direct interactions like `Request Endorsement`, `Private Meeting`, and the `Boost/Reduce Favorability` or `Boost/Reduce Influence` actions. Stat-shaping actions cost both actions and campaign funds.
- **Post news** — Publish commentary on the `/news` page; build a following through engagement.
- **Earn achievements** — Winning elections, building donor networks, and posting news unlock profile badges. Achievements display as a tile grid on your profile, organized by category.
- **Form a coalition** — If you're a national party chair, create a cross-party coalition to signal political alignment with other parties. Invite parties or accept join requests.
- **Set a campaign song** — Link a YouTube video in Settings → Campaign Song; it plays on your public profile.
- **Follow politicians** — Subscribe to other characters to track their careers.
- **Portfolio** — View your stock and bond holdings; send wire transfers from here.
- **Found a corporation** — Start a business, expand into state markets, issue shares and bonds, and manage sector production strategies.

## US-Specific: Presidential Track

Presidential candidates can **Travel** to a US state during the general election (5 actions). While traveling, you earn +1% Favorability per turn passively. Your travel state is shown on the electoral map as a badge so other players can see where you're campaigning. NPI (not state PI) drives presidential vote potential — build it over time by keeping state influence high.

## UK-Specific: Downing Street

UK players compete for seats in the **House of Commons** via multi-seat proportional elections. After each Commons cycle resolves nationally, the largest-party leader attempts to form a government. MPs can trigger or vote on **confidence motions** against the Prime Minister. Use the UK Government hub at `/executive/uk` for current seat totals, government status, and confidence votes.

## Tips

- Join a party early — independents can't run for office
- Campaign in your home state; it costs less and builds influence fastest
- Let actions accumulate before spending big (polls, donor base)
- Watch the [Election History](/wiki/elections) to understand how races have played out
- Your Infamy decays on its own — don't worry too much if it spikes briefly
